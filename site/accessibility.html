Inclusivity · Digital Standards

Accessibility Statement

Ensuring our digital world, sensory notes, and commerce are warm, legible, and navigable for everyone.

Conformance Goal: WCAG 2.1 Level AA · Section 508 Guidelines

At VIRAI, we believe sensory storytelling should be open to all. We are committed to digital accessibility and continuously optimizing our storefront for individuals using screen readers, keyboard-only navigation, screen magnifiers, and voice-command technologies.

01 · Structural Markup & Screen Readers

Semantic Code Hierarchy

Our pages are built with valid, semantic HTML5 landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>, and <aside>). Heading levels follow an orderly mathematical progression, form controls have explicit programmatic labels, and interactive buttons feature ARIA states reflecting disclosure status and cart counts.

02 · Keyboard Navigation & Focus

Unconstrained Tab Order

Every interactive element—including navigation menus, shopping bag drawer toggles, fragrance selectors, and the checkout funnel—can be navigated and activated using a standard keyboard alone. Visible, high-contrast focus rings are preserved, and bypass "Skip to content" links are available at the top of every page.

03 · Color Contrast & Typography

Optical Legibility

We maintain strict WCAG AA contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display titles) across our palette of warm rice, mineral charcoal, and muted earth tones. We avoid using color alone to convey meaning or transactional errors.

04 · Reduced Motion & Sensory Control

Mindful Visuals

We honor the prefers-reduced-motion operating system setting. For visitors sensitive to visual transitions, ambient animations, smooth zooms, and interactive canvas particles are gracefully disabled.

05 · Fragrance Descriptions as Alt Text

Descriptive Sensory Imagery

All product photography, vessel glaze studies, and botanical accord graphics feature meaningful alternative text (alt) explaining the visual scene, wax vessel proportions, and botanical ingredients.
