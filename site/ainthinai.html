The Five Landscapes of Love

In classical Tamil poetry, love was never one feeling — it was five, each belonging to a landscape.

01 குறிஞ்சி

The Mountains · 01

Kurinji

Union

Where love begins. Steep hillsides, cool air, and flowers that bloom once in many years — a landscape for the moment two people choose each other.

Explore Kurinji

02 முல்லை

The Forests · 02

Mullai

Waiting

Jasmine that opens only at night. The forest knows how to wait without bitterness — for the traveller, the promise, the return.

Explore Mullai

03 மருதம்

The River Country · 03

Marutham

Playful Conflict

Full summer. Fields heavy with grain and quarrels that end in laughter — love at its loudest, most alive hour.

Explore Marutham

04 நெய்தல்

The Coast · 04

Neithal

Longing

Salt wind and grey water. The landscape of missing someone who is still yours — loving across distance, faithfully.

Explore Neithal

05 பாலை

The Dry Lands · 05

Palai

Separation

Sun-scorched earth under a white sky. Absence held with dignity — the hardest landscape, and proof of what endures.
