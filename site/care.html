Support · Product Care

Care, in five small rituals.

A candle asks for very little and returns a great deal. These five habits keep yours burning beautifully to its final evening.

01 - The first burn

Let it reach the edge

The first lighting is the most important. Allow the wax to melt fully across the vessel's surface - one to two patient hours - before extinguishing. Wax has memory; teach it well early.

02 - The trim

Five millimetres, every time

Before each lighting, trim the wick to about 5 mm with scissors or a wick trimmer. A short wick burns low and clean; a long one smokes and hurries the wax.

03 - The session

Hours, not afternoons

Burn no longer than four hours at a stretch, and never leave a flame unattended. Keep it away from drafts, curtains, children and curious pets.

04 - The rest

Between burnings

Extinguish by dipping the wick into melted wax with a snuffer or spoon - it prevents smoke and keeps the wax scented. Let the vessel cool fully before moving it.

05 - The second life

The vessel remains

When roughly half a centimetre of wax remains, let the candle rest. Warm the vessel gently, pour out the last wax, wipe clean - and keep the vessel for brushes, spices or jasmine stems.
