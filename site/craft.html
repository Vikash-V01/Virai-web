Craft & Materials

Nothing loud.
Nothing wasted.

Every material in a Virai object was chosen slowly, and can be explained plainly.

A candle is a simple technology: wax, wick, vessel, fragrance. Its quality is decided by how honestly each part is chosen - and by what it leaves behind in the air of your home.

I - The Wax

A coconut–soy blend, chosen for the burn

We blend coconut and soy waxes for a clean, even melt pool and a low, steady flame. It is a slower burn than paraffin - deliberately. Fragrance should unfold across hours, not announce itself once.

II - The Wick

Cotton, lead-free, trimmed for you to keep trimming

Woven cotton wicks, sized to each vessel. A five-millimetre trim before each lighting keeps the flame quiet and the wax honest.

III - The Vessel

Meant to outlive the wax

Stoneware-toned vessels with weight and warmth in the hand. When the wax ends, the vessel does not - wash it warm, keep brushes or rings or jasmine in it. Discarding it would be the only wasteful act here.

IV - The Fragrance

Built from the landscape up

Each composition begins as a place and its emotion, translated note by note: top, heart, base. Descriptions on our pages are written from the blends themselves - sensory honesty is a material too.

V - The Paper

Wrapping that returns gently

Rice-paper wraps, cotton cord, uncoated cards. Tactile, minimal, and kinder at the end of its life than gloss ever is.
