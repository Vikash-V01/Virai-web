Trust · Digital Stewardship

Privacy & Data Rights

How VIRAI collects, uses, and safeguards your personal details with quiet restraint and uncompromising care.

Effective Date: September 2026 · Compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023

At VIRAI, we approach data stewardship with the same mindful minimalism that governs our botanical candle formulations. We gather only the minimal information essential to fulfill your bespoke orders, safeguard your account, and deliver a serene patron experience. We never sell, monetize, or broker your personal information.

01 · Information We Collect

Data Gathered with Clear Purpose

Depending on how you interact with VIRAI, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Patron & Delivery Details: Name, shipping address, email address, and phone number provided during checkout or account registration to fulfill and track your orders.
  • Authentication & Account Data: Securely salted credentials or 6-digit one-time passcodes (OTP) dispatched for passwordless email access.
  • Google Sign-In (OAuth 2.0): When you choose to authenticate using Google, we receive only your verified email address, public profile name, and account identifier from Google Identity Services. We do not access, view, or request your Google account password, contacts, Drive files, or emails.
  • Transactional Records: Order references, consignment identifiers, items selected, and invoice totals required under Indian GST statutory mandates.
  • Essential Technical Data: Masked IP addresses used strictly for rate-limiting, brute-force defense, and fraud prevention.
02 · Payment Security & Zero PAN Retention

Direct Bank-Grade Tokenization

Your payment credentials are never captured, processed, or saved on VIRAI servers. All payments are handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors (such as Razorpay or Stripe) compliant with Reserve Bank of India (RBI) tokenization guidelines. We only receive a confirmation of transaction status and a gateway payment reference.

03 · How We Use Your Data

Fulfillment, Communication & Security

We process your personal information solely for legitimate commercial and fulfillment purposes:

  • To blend, pack, and courier your botanical orders to your requested destination.
  • To transmit dispatch milestones, waybill consignment numbers, and customer care responses.
  • To maintain your patron account, order archives, and saved shipping addresses for convenient reordering.
  • To safeguard our digital storefront from malicious activity, denial-of-service attempts, and automated spam.
04 · Third-Party Services & Logistics Partners

Strict Disclosures Only for Fulfillment

We share specific, necessary data exclusively with trusted operational partners under strict confidentiality obligations:

  • Logistics Couriers: Your recipient name, physical address, and contact telephone are shared with delivery partners (e.g., Delhivery, Blue Dart) solely to complete doorstep delivery.
  • Google Identity Services: Used optionally for instant, secure authentication via Google OAuth 2.0. Google processes authentication in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Transactional Communications: Encrypted mail delivery gateways used solely to send order invoices and one-time access codes.
05 · Your Statutory Rights Under the DPDP Act 2023

Patron Autonomy & Data Rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and internationally recognized privacy principles, you enjoy comprehensive rights over your personal data:

  • Right of Access & Summary: You may request a complete summary of your personal information held in our records at any time.
  • Right to Correction & Erasure: You may request the update of inaccurate details or the complete deletion of your account and personal identifiers, subject to statutory tax record retention requirements.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Where processing is predicated on consent, you may withdraw your consent at any time without retroactive penalty.
  • Right to Nominate: You have the right to nominate an individual who, in the event of death or incapacity, may exercise data rights on your behalf.
06 · Cookies & Local Storage

Transparent Client Preferences

We use minimal local storage and essential session cookies solely to preserve your active shopping bag contents, retain your patron login session, and remember your cookie consent preferences. We do not employ intrusive cross-site third-party tracking beacons.

07 · Data Retention & Disposal

Scheduled Minimization

We retain your personal information only as long as required to maintain your patron account or fulfill statutory obligations. Commercial invoices and tax receipts are archived for the mandatory duration specified under Indian taxation laws, after which customer identifiers are permanently deleted or irreversibly pseudonymized.
