Trust · Engineering Standards

Security & Responsible Disclosure

How we safeguard transactional privacy, encrypt communications, and collaborate with security researchers.

Standards: TLS 1.3 · RBI Tokenization Guidelines · DPDP Act 2023

Security at VIRAI is treated as an essential craft discipline. We safeguard our customers' digital footsteps with defense-in-depth architecture, strict data minimization, and automated isolation controls.

01 · Transport & Header Defenses

Strict Encryption in Transit

All network interactions are strictly enforced over modern TLS with HTTP Strict Transport Security (HSTS, max-age 31536000). We enforce granular Content Security Policies (CSP), anti-clickjacking frame controls, Referrer-Policy protections, and Permissions-Policy lockdowns disabling camera, microphone, and geolocation access.

02 · Payment Isolation & Zero PAN Storage

PCI-DSS & RBI Compliance

We never capture, view, or store raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or netbanking credentials on our servers. All financial transactions are processed via RBI-licensed and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways with device tokenization.

03 · Data Minimization & Anonymization

Statutory Data Lifecycle

In adherence to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, customer delivery details are retained only for active fulfillment, returns, and statutory tax reconciliation. Historical transactional PII is subject to automated redaction routines, separating mandatory ledger records from customer personal identifiers.

04 · Rate Limiting & Anti-Abuse Controls

API & Brute-Force Safeguards

All public API routes, coupon application endpoints, authentication attempts, and checkout computations operate behind sliding-window rate limiters with exponential lockouts to eliminate brute-force enumeration and automated abuse.

COORDINATED VULNERABILITY DISCLOSURE

Responsible Disclosure Guidelines

We welcome security researchers and ethical technologists who help keep our infrastructure and customer data resilient. If you discover a potential vulnerability across any VIRAI property:

  • Report Promptly: Transmit your findings privately to security@virai.in with detailed proof-of-concept steps.
  • Protect User Privacy: Do not access, modify, or exfiltrate real customer data or disrupt production service availability.
  • Responsible Embargo: Allow our engineering team a minimum of 30 calendar days to triage and remediate the issue prior to public disclosure.
  • Safe Harbor: VIRAI pledges not to pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith who comply with these responsible disclosure parameters.
